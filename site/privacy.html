Privacy

Short version: your sightings are yours, and they stay on your PC. No account, no tracking, no adverts.

What stays on your PC

Everything you put into Thicket: sightings, photos, places, outings, notes and backups. It's kept in a folder on your own computer, and Thicket never uploads it anywhere. There's no Thicket account and no Thicket server.

When Thicket goes online

Only for these three things, only over a secure connection, and only to these services:

WhatServiceWhat it's told
The map's background OpenFreeMap Which map areas you're looking at, as with any web map. Not your sightings: those are drawn on your PC.
US species packs GitHub Nothing about you: Thicket fetches the list of packs and the pack you pick, only when you add a state.
iNaturalist photos iNaturalist The numbers of your iNaturalist observations, only when you import an export and tick Also download the photos.

Like any website you visit, these services see your internet (IP) address. Thicket sends nothing else: no usage statistics, no crash reports, and no update checks.

To turn all of it off, untick Settings › Online features › Allow. Thicket then works fully offline, with a plain outline map.

When you share your records

Exports only happen when you make one, and you choose what's in it. When exporting to share, Thicket can blur exact locations, and it removes where photos were taken (their GPS) unless you say otherwise.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no adverts, and it doesn't load fonts or scripts from anywhere else. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses; see the GitHub privacy statement.

The download button takes you to GitHub, where Thicket's installer is published.

Questions

Ask on GitHub. Thicket's source code is public there too, so anyone can check what it does.
