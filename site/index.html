Why Thicket

  • Quick to record

    Start typing a name and pick the species. Today's date, the time and your last place fill themselves in, so a sighting takes seconds.

  • Yours, on your PC

    Your sightings and photos stay on your own computer, backed up automatically. No account, no cloud, no adverts.

  • UK and US species

    Every UK species is built in. Recording in the United States? Each state's species pack downloads in a few seconds.

Ready to start your journal?

Already keep records on iNaturalist or in a spreadsheet? Thicket imports them, iNaturalist photos included.
